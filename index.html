FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I order a product through this website?

Yes. The website includes a sample order form. It is designed for demonstration and does not save orders in a database.

Will my order information be stored?

No. The order form only keeps the information temporarily in the browser while you complete the demonstration. Refreshing the page clears it.

Are the products and prices final?

No. The products, prices, and descriptions are sample content and can be replaced with your organization's actual information.

Does this website process real payments?

No. It is a front-end educational website. The checkout confirms a sample order but does not charge a card or connect to a payment provider.
